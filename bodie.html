Nags Head

Built in 1872, the Bodie Island Lighthouse is operated by the National Park Service. Interior view looking upward. Panoramic view from the top. This lighthouse was built to help guide ships through a very dangerous passage through the Diamond Shoals which extend out into the Atlantic for 15 miles. 650 ships have been lost in the Outer Banks. The lighthouse still has the 1st order Fresnel lens. The present day Bodie Island lighthouse is the third construction of the lighthouse. The second one was destroyed by the Confederate Army to prevent it from being used by the Union during the Civil War.
